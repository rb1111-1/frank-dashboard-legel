# Frank House Dashboard Notes — SMS Opt-In

Frank House Dashboard is a private home-notification application. Authorized household members can
send a short note to the household display by text message, and the application replies with an SMS
confirmation that the note was received and displayed.

## How to opt in

To start using the service, **text the word `START` to the Frank House Dashboard number** provided to
you by the account owner. By texting the number, you agree to receive SMS confirmation replies from
the Frank House Dashboard application.

- **What you'll receive:** an SMS confirmation each time you submit a note (a few messages per month).
- **Message frequency:** low — typically only a few messages per month.
- **Message and data rates may apply.**
- **Opt out any time** by replying **STOP**. Reply **HELP** for help.

## Consent

This service is operated by a private individual for personal and household use. Only the account
owner and household members they authorize use the service. No third parties are messaged, and mobile
information is never shared with third parties for marketing or other use.
