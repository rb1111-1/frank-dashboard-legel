# Privacy Policy — Frank House Dashboard Notes

_Last updated: 2026-08-27_

This Privacy Policy describes how the Frank House Dashboard Notes service ("the Service," "we,"
"our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with its SMS text-message feature.

## What the Service does

The Service lets a household member send a short text note by SMS to a dedicated phone number. The
note is displayed on a private in-home e-paper dashboard. The Service sends back a brief SMS
confirmation that the note was received. The Service is operated by a private individual for personal
and family use. It is not a commercial product and does not message the general public.

## Information we collect

When you send a text message to our number, we receive and process:

- **Your mobile phone number**, as provided by the mobile carrier when you send a message.
- **The content of the message** you send (the note text).
- **Message metadata** such as the date and time the message was sent.

We do **not** collect your name, location, contacts, photos, or any information beyond the message
you choose to send and the phone number it was sent from.

## How we use the information

- To display the note you sent on the private in-home dashboard.
- To send you an SMS confirmation that your note was received.
- To operate, maintain, and troubleshoot the Service.

The current note is stored only until it is replaced by a newer note or cleared. We do not build
profiles, run analytics, or use the information for advertising.

## How we share information

We do **not** sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for their own use. Information
is processed only by the infrastructure needed to run the Service:

- **Twilio** (our SMS provider) transmits messages to and from our number, subject to
  [Twilio's privacy policy](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/legal/privacy).
- **Home Assistant**, a private, self-hosted home-automation system we control, stores the current
  note so the dashboard can display it.

No message content or phone numbers are disclosed to anyone outside the household operating the
Service, except as required by law.

## Data retention

Only the most recent note is retained; it is overwritten when a new note is sent or cleared. Message
records held by our SMS provider are retained according to that provider's own retention policies.

## Your choices

- **Opt out at any time** by replying **STOP** to our number. You will receive no further messages.
- Reply **HELP** for assistance and information about the Service.
- To request that we delete information associated with your phone number, contact us using the
  information below.

## Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the information the Service handles, including provider-level
transport security and access controls on our self-hosted system. No method of transmission or
storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Children's privacy

The Service is intended for use by the household that operates it and is not directed to children
under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating
the "Last updated" date above.

## Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be directed to the Service operator at the contact address
provided during account registration.
