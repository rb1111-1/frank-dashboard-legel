# Frank House Dashboard — SMS Consent Form (Call to Action)

_This page is the publicly viewable **Call-to-Action (CTA)** and consent disclosure for the Frank
House Dashboard SMS service, provided for A2P 10DLC campaign verification._

---

## Program

**Frank House Dashboard** is a private, personal home-automation notification service operated by an
individual (sole proprietor) for their own household. Participants are limited to the account owner
and the immediate family members the owner authorizes. The service is not offered to the public, and
no marketing or promotional messages are ever sent.

## Call to Action — how a participant opts in

A participant opts in by **texting the keyword `START` from their own mobile phone to the Frank
House Dashboard number, +1 (651) 661-0582**. Sending that text is the participant's own affirmative
opt-in action. No one is ever enrolled by another person, no phone numbers are imported or purchased,
and the service never sends a message to a number that has not first texted it.

> **By texting START to +1 (651) 661-0582, you agree to receive transactional SMS confirmation
> messages from Frank House Dashboard.** Consent is voluntary and is not required to use the home
> dashboard. Message frequency varies (typically a few messages per month). Message and data rates
> may apply. Reply **STOP** at any time to opt out; reply **HELP** for help. See our
> [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md) and [Terms of Service](./terms-of-service.md).

## What you receive after opting in

- An SMS **confirmation reply** each time you send a note to the dashboard.
- **Message frequency varies** (typically a few messages per month).
- **Message and data rates may apply.**

## Opting out

- Reply **STOP** at any time to unsubscribe and receive no further messages.
- Reply **HELP** for help.

## Disclosures

- Messages are transactional confirmations only. **No marketing or promotional messages** are sent.
- **No mobile information is shared with any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional
  purposes.**
- Recipients are limited to the account owner and their opted-in family members. No third parties are
  messaged.
